# day-count

How a year of interest is counted. Actual/365 (ACT/365F): the real number of days divided by 365 — the
convention for US dollar fixed coupons. 30/360: months counted as 30 days and a year as 360, the bond
market's tidy convention. Same rate, different conventions, slightly different accruals; state the term
in whole quarters when the coupon schedule, not a formula, sets the payment.
