# estimated-value

The issuer's own estimate of what the note is worth to an investor as of the pricing date, computed
without any distribution charges — the price at which the note would change hands between professionals.
It is always stated against the issue price (e.g. dollars per $10 of stated principal) and is lower than
the public offering price: the gap is the fee drag of underwriting, structuring and hedging. It is not a
guarantee, nor a minimum resale price.
