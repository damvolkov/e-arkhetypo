# autocall

An automatic call (a "kick-out") ends a structured note early: on an Observation Date whose closing level
reaches the call threshold — often the Initial Value — the issuer must redeem the note at par plus the
coupon due, and no further amounts are owed. An autocallable is typically not callable on the first
observation dates nor on the final one; the ladder of potential call dates and their payouts follows the
Observation schedule.
