# contingent-coupon

A Contingent Coupon is a periodic interest payment on a structured note that is due **only if** the
underlying closes at or above a stated Coupon Barrier on the related Observation Date. When the level is
below the barrier, no coupon accrues or is paid for that period — it is lost, not deferred. On quarterly
notes the per-note amount is the annual rate divided by four, times the stated principal per note.
