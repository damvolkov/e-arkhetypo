# eusipa

EUSIPA — the European Structured Investment Products Association — publishes a "derivative map" that
sorts retail structured products into families: Capital Protected, Yield Enhancement, Participation,
Reference Entity, Leveraged (with or without Knock-Out) and Constant Leverage products (map dated
2020-02-13). A note that pays a fixed contingent coupon, is callable above par and repays principal only
subject to a downside threshold is a **Yield Enhancement** product — it sacrifices full participation
for periodic income and carries capital at risk; EUSIPA assigns no official numeric code to US shelf
notes, only the family name.
